Before you book

Good to know

Where do you braid?
Come to my home in Duluth, or I come to you (a $20 travel fee applies). I share my exact address once your deposit is paid.
How should I prepare?
Please have your hair washed, detangled and blow-dried. I braid only, so I do not wash hair or apply chemicals.
What about the deposit?
$20 holds your spot and counts toward the total.
Can I reschedule?
Please give 24 hours notice. Without it the deposit is not returned.
Is braiding hair included?
Braiding hair is not included in the price. This is the hair I recommend you buy: Outre X-Pression pre-stretched braiding hair, 42 inch
How long will it take?
It depends on size and length. The times on this page are a guide, from about 2 hours for jumbo to about 6 hours for small.
